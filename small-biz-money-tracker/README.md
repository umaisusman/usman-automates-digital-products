**You can't grow what you don't track.**

Freelancers and solopreneurs lose money in the same quiet way: income scattered across apps, expenses forgotten by tax time, and no clear answer to "am I actually profitable this month?" Bookkeeping software is overkill — and overpriced — when you're a team of one.

**The Small Business Money Tracker** is a complete money system in a single Excel workbook. Log income and expenses as they happen; the dashboard computes your monthly profit & loss automatically, and the tax summary keeps your deductible expenses organized with quarterly estimated-tax helpers. No subscription, no account, no app — just a file you own forever.

### What's inside

- **INCOME log** — date, client/source, amount, category dropdown (Services, Products, Affiliate, Other); space for 500 entries
- **EXPENSES log** — date, vendor, amount, category dropdown (Software, Marketing, Supplies, Travel, Professional services, Other), plus a tax-deductible Yes/No flag
- **DASHBOARD** — monthly profit & loss auto-computed from your logs: income, expenses, profit, and margin % for all 12 months, year totals, an income-vs-expenses bar chart, and income/expense breakdowns by category
- **TAX SUMMARY** — deductible expense totals by category (only counts what you flagged deductible), plus a quarterly estimated-tax helper: enter your tax rate, see a rough payment figure per quarter
- **README sheet** — clear setup and usage instructions
- **Example rows included** — clearly labeled, delete them and add your own
- **Works everywhere** — plain Excel formulas throughout; imports cleanly into Google Sheets

### Who this is for

Freelancers, solopreneurs, and side-hustlers who want to know their numbers without paying for bookkeeping software — writers, designers, developers, coaches, consultants, creators.
