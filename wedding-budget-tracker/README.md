**Weddings are beautiful. Wedding spreadsheets are not — but they save you thousands.**

Between the venue, the caterer, the photographer, and the forty-seventh "quick question" from a vendor, costs blur and deadlines slip. Couples who track everything in one place stay on budget and actually enjoy the planning. Couples who don't... find out at the final invoice.

**The Wedding Budget & Planning Tracker** is a complete Excel workbook with four connected sheets that run your entire wedding: money, vendors, guests, and timeline. Yellow cells are yours to fill in — everything else calculates itself.

### What's inside

- **BUDGET sheet** — 17 wedding categories (venue, catering, photo, video, attire, flowers, music, cake, and more) with Planned vs. Actual columns, auto-calculating Variance (red when you're over), and a TOTAL row
- **VENDOR PAYMENTS sheet** — every vendor contract in one place: total, deposit paid, balance due (auto), due date, and status dropdown — sort by due date a month out and nothing slips
- **GUEST LIST sheet** — 100 guest rows with Side, RSVP status, and +1 dropdowns; Headcount counts Accepted guests automatically; TOTALS row gives you the final number for your caterer
- **TIMELINE CHECKLIST sheet** — 30 tasks ordered from 12 months out to the wedding day, with Done/Pending dropdowns and a live PROGRESS percentage bar
- **Quick-start guide (PDF)** — one page explaining each sheet so you're up and running in 10 minutes
- **Works everywhere** — plain Excel formulas throughout; also imports cleanly into Google Sheets

### Who this is for

Engaged couples planning their own wedding who want one organized place for the budget, the vendors, the guest list, and the timeline — without paying for planning software or a month-to-month app.
